# Masking social interactions

What does it mean to mask

Autistic people do know  how to lie
- There's an idea that autistic people are _beings of lights_, incapable of lying, or to manipulate reality
- We do know how to lie, all humans beings lie. We don't do it to hurt, but we the purpose to deflect conflicts
- To learn to adapt, 

I've lied, yes... I've said that I had a job, when in reality I was unemployed.
I've said that I wanted to hang out when in reality I didn't even wanted to go to that place.

To lie while telling the truth it must be one the must difficult things to do, _at least for me_. It's an incredible form of manipulation that
People have done it to me, and it feels horrible. If you want to bring that up and accuse  the other person it's impossible. 

It's called __paltering__, which is the active use of selective truthful statements to mislead
