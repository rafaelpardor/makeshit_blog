# Class-Responsibility-Collaboration Card

Use Class, responsibilities, and Collaboration Cards to design the system as a team. The biggest value of CRC cards is to allow people to break away from the procedural mode of though and more fully appreciate object technology.
The more people who can help design the system the greater the number of good ideas incorporated.

## Collaboration Ownership


A Class Responsibility Collaboration(CRC) card is a design tool used in OOP to identify classes, their responsibilities, and their interactions with other classes in a software system.
