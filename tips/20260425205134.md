
How To Win In Software Engineering!!!!! 
1. Read code more than you speak. 
2. Ship small, consistent improvements. 
3. Learn to debug properly, not just patch things. 
4. Understand the business, not just the ticket. 
5. Write clear docs for what you build. 
6. Ask why before jumping into code. 
7. Get good at system design and tradeoffs. 
8. Make reliability a habit, not an afterthought. 
9. Communicate risks early, do not hide delays. 
10. Keep learning outside your current stack.

Sometimes they will try to gaslight you into thinking bad planning, unclear requirements, and unrealistic timelines are your fault. Do not give in.
