# Kubernetes

Here is why you must use ResourceQuota and LimitRange in your kubernetes namespace. 
1. Stop one app from using all CPU and memory
2. Make sure every pod has safe resource limits
3. Prevent pods from running without resource settings
4. Protect your cluster from crashes during high usage
5. Keep your apps stable and predictable
6. Avoid one workload affecting others 

- ResourceQuota controls the total usage 
- LimitRange controls each pod
