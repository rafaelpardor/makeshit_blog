90% of SQL interviews in 2026 come down to these 7 points. Can you do them without guessing? 
1) Know joins cold: inner vs left, when a WHERE turns a left join into an inner join 
2) Aggregation rules: GROUP BY, HAVING vs WHERE, counting distinct without blowing up rows 
3) Window functions: ROW_NUMBER vs RANK, running totals, dedupe with PARTITION BY 
4) Null semantics: NULL in comparisons, COALESCE, NOT IN pitfalls, 3-valued logic 
5) Performance basics: indexes, sargable predicates, why leading wildcards kill btree usage 
6) Read an EXPLAIN: spot seq scan vs index scan, bad cardinality, missing/unused indexes 
7) Data correctness: transactions, isolation levels, idempotent writes, why read committed can still surprise you
