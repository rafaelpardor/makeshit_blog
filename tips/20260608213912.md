Kafka vs RabbitMQ vs NATS: picking a queue by workload, not vibes. 

Kafka 
1) Best for event streams + replay (days of retention, consumer offsets) 
2) High throughput, partitions = scale lever (and ordering constraint) 
3) Tradeoffs: ops heavy, schema + compaction choices matter, exactly-once is tricky 

RabbitMQ 
1) Best for work queues + routing (topics, fanout, dead-letter, priorities) 
2) Per-message ack + prefetch gives tight backpressure control 
3) Tradeoffs: large backlogs hurt, replay is manual, throughput lower than Kafka at scale 

NATS 
1) Best for low-latency pub/sub + RPC-ish messaging; JetStream adds persistence 
2) Simple to run, great for edge/microservices, fast fanout 
3) Tradeoffs: fewer ecosystem patterns than Kafka, persistence semantics vary by config (streams, replicas, limits)
