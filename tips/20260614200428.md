Backend Interview Question: Kafka topic partitioned by user_id. Problem: One user generates massive traffic Single partition overloaded How do you redesign partitioning to avoid hotspots?

Classic Kafka gotcha: same key = same partition = hotspots on power users. Fix: salt the key (user_id + hash % N) for even spread, or use a separate topic for high-volume users. Ordering stays intact per salted sub-key if you design it right. Always profile your key distribution!
