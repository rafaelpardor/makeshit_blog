
UNWRITTEN RULES OF SOFTWARE ENGINEER SURVIVAL:
1. Do not discuss your salary with random coworkers at work. 
2. Always write things down. 
3. Good work matters, but visibility matters too. 
4. Never act smarter than everyone in the room. 
5. Learn who actually decides promotions. 
6. Do not gossip in team chats, lunch groups, or coffee breaks. 
7. Reply with clarity, not with essays. 
8. Over-communicate on important work. 
9. Do not attach your ego to one stack. 
10. Keep a private brag document. 
11. Do not burn bridges when leaving. 
12. Learn to manage up. 
13. Never promise timelines just to look good. 
14. Protect your energy. 
15. Your manager is not your best friend.
